Foliora Studio — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

Foliora Studio is a Microsoft Word add-in published by Foliora. This policy explains what information the add-in handles and why.

Information handled

When you create or use a Foliora account, the service may process account information such as your email address, account identifier, display name, authentication state, subscription or entitlement state, and feature-usage quota. Authentication, entitlement, and quota services are provided through Supabase.

Word document content

Formatting and conversion features operate on the document content needed for the feature you explicitly run. The current OCR feature is different: when you choose an image for OCR, the selected image data is sent to Foliora's server endpoint so the requested extraction can be performed.

AI OCR processing

Foliora routes OCR requests through its server infrastructure to Google Gemini. The Gemini API key is stored server-side and is never requested from, exposed to, or stored in the Word add-in. OCR output is returned to the add-in so you can review it before inserting it into Word.

Local storage

The add-in may store non-document preferences and cached application state in browser/local storage, including workspace preferences and locally cached entitlement or OCR-quota display information. Server-side entitlement and quota state remains authoritative.

Third-party services

Foliora uses Supabase for authentication and service-state operations and Google Gemini for AI OCR. If you choose an upgrade option, the add-in may open an external Foliora checkout page and pass the account information needed to identify the selected plan. Review the applicable third-party terms before completing a purchase.

Security

Network requests to Foliora services use HTTPS. Authentication tokens are sent only to services required for the requested account or OCR operation. Do not submit confidential material to an AI feature unless you are authorized to do so.

Data sharing and advertising

Foliora does not sell document content to advertisers. Data is transmitted to third-party services only when required to provide a feature you explicitly invoke or a service operation you initiate.

Your choices

You can stop using a feature, sign out of the add-in, or stop using the service at any time. For account-data access, correction, deletion, or privacy questions, contact Foliora through the support page.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the Foliora Studio Support page.
